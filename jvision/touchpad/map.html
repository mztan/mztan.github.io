Riverside Museum is a purple building on the south bank of the Lark River, at Dock Ave and Harbor St. Drag the map (or scroll with two fingers) to pan, and pinch (or use − and +) to zoom; pinch zooms around the cursor and the buttons zoom around the center. Street names and entrances (▲) appear when you're zoomed in far enough. Click a marker to see what it is, then select Choose this landmark. Reset view goes back to the start. Timing starts when you select Start.
